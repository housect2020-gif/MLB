What this is: a filter, not a prediction model. Every game this season is classified by how decisive the head-to-head record between the two teams is — a real 3+ game H2H lead ("Strong"), a 1-2 game lead ("Moderate"), or a tied series decided only by season-record tiebreak ("Coinflip"). The backtest numbers below are computed live against every completed game this season, so they update as the season goes. Stacking multiple legs into a parlay multiplies the odds against you even when each individual pick is strong — see the note under "Combos" before reading too much into the combined percentage. Not real sportsbook odds, not betting advice — entertainment only.
